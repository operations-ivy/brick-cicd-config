# Shaders

`crt-pi.glslp` and `shaders/crt-pi.glsl`: crt-pi, "a Raspberry Pi friendly CRT
shader", Copyright (C) 2015-2016 davej, GNU General Public License version 2
or later (see the header of `crt-pi.glsl`). From libretro's glsl-shaders,
commit 148ce728155232a6b63be772c1810bd84728f359 (`crt/crt-pi.glslp`,
`crt/shaders/crt-pi.glsl`), unchanged. brick-retro passes it to RetroArch
(`--set-shader`) for every core.
